GOOD ART.
BAD INTENT.

A 2–6 player game of prop hunt in a dark museum: thieves hide as furniture, cops search with flashlights.

1. Get everyone into a room

  1. Choose a nickname and create a room. Share its four-letter code with friends.
  2. The host chooses a museum in the lobby: the original Museum of Questionable Taste, Velvet Vault, Midnight Archive, or Random. These are different layouts of the same museum.
  3. Everyone readies up. The host starts the round. Changing the map resets readiness.

Teams are random every round: one cop for 2–5 players, two cops for six. Everyone else is a thief. Late arrivals watch until the next round.

2. If you are a thief

  • You get a 30-second head start while the cops keep their eyes shut. Move, find treasure, and find a hiding spot.
  • You can see the whole museum, and the cops’ flashlight beams. Cops can only see what their beams light up.
  • Press Q near a highlighted prop to copy it. Press again to become a thief. Disguises move slower. Inside a beam, freeze: a prop that moves is a dead giveaway.
  • Sprinting (Space) is loud. Cops see footstep ripples near where you are, but not who you are.
  • Press E near treasure to pick it up. Carry two small items, worth $200 each.
  • A $700 painting is faster with two carriers. First carrier steers; second assists. Either presses R to drop it. You cannot disguise while carrying a painting.
  • Deposit at the van with E. Once the displayed crew quota is met, interact again empty-handed to escape.

Win: meet the quota and extract every free, connected thief. At least one must escape. Captured or disconnected teammates can be left behind.

2. If you are a cop

  • Keep your eyes closed for the 30-second head start. Then the lights stay off: you only see what your flashlight lights up, plus a faint floor plan.
  • Aim with the mouse (on a keyboard or phone, the beam follows your movement). Walls block the beam.
  • Press E or click to arrest the exact object your beam points at; it gets a blue ring. Get close: arrests only reach a short way.
  • Catching a thief has a 2-second cooldown. Arresting a real prop is a miss with a 5-second cooldown, and everyone sees it.
  • Gold ripples are footsteps: a thief sprinting nearby.
  • Cops cannot disguise or steal. There is no cop Q action.
  • The van apron is protected from arrest. Stop thieves before they get there.

Win: arrest all connected thieves before they escape, or hold out until the seven-minute lockdown timer expires.

3. Getting caught is not always the end

Captured thieves go to the holding room. A free teammate can stand near its lock, press E, and stay still for three seconds to rescue everyone. Moving cancels the rescue. Rescued thieves receive a five-second green shield against immediate re-arrest. If all connected thieves are captured, the round ends immediately.

Controls and phones

Space / Sprint button: a 1.4-second burst of speed, with an 8-second cooldown. Both teams can sprint, but thieves must drop paintings and leave their disguise first. Explore the expanded east wing: Conservatory, Royal Collection, Restoration Studio and Antiquities.

WASD / arrow keys: move · Mouse: aim your flashlight (cops) · E or click: interact, or arrest as a cop · Q: disguise (thieves only) · R: drop loot (thieves only).

On a phone, rotate to landscape. Move with the left joystick and use the action buttons on the right. The ? menu also lets desktop players turn on touch controls. Sound is optional.

Between rounds

The winning team sees a celebration; the losing team sees its own defeat screen. The host returns everyone to the lobby to select another map. Ready up again for new random teams.

Refreshing the same tab restores your identity. Disconnecting drops carried loot. The clock keeps running while a tab is backgrounded. Empty rooms expire after two minutes.

Friends need the same reachable game URL. On another device, localhost refers to that device—not the computer hosting your game.
